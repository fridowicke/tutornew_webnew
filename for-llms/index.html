tutor.new: AI-Powered Tutoring with Real Rewards

Overview

tutor.new is a Swiss educational technology platform that provides personalized AI tutoring. It is designed to be a patient, 24/7 learning companion for students across various subjects and educational levels.

Core Value Proposition

  • Personalized Learning: AI adapts to the student's pace and style.
  • Motivation through Rewards: A unique "Learn-to-Earn" system where students earn coins for progress, redeemable for physical rewards like Apple AirPods or gadgets.
  • Affordability: High-quality tutoring at a fraction of the cost of traditional human tutors.

How It Works

  1. Input: Students upload photos of their homework or describe what they don't understand.
  2. Interaction: The AI engages in a (voice) chat, guiding the student toward the solution rather than just giving the answer.
  3. Reward: Completion of learning units grants coins.
  4. Redemption: Coins are exchanged for real rewards sent to the student's home.

Technical & Didactic Approach

The platform uses Large Language Models optimized for educational purposes, focusing on Socratic questioning. It provides automatic lecture notes, quizzes, and interactive visualizations to reinforce learning.

Founders & Vision

Founded by Gero and Friedrich, mathematicians and computer scientists based in Zurich. Their vision is to use software to provide high-quality, personalized education to every student worldwide, regardless of their socioeconomic background.
