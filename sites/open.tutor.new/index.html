OpenTutor powers 1:1 learning for everyone

OpenTutor is an open-source AI tutor for 1:1 learning.
The best way to learn has always been individual instruction. Now we have the technology to make it scale. OpenTutor gives anyone access to a personalized tutor, and makes the entire system open, inspectable, and modifiable. Every line of code is public. You can run it yourself, adapt it to your needs, and share your improvements.
OpenTutor is built by teachers and learners around the world, coordinated in Switzerland.
